## Description
Ever opened your phone on a normal day and suddenly felt like your life wasn't good enough?

In this video we break down the psychology behind social comparison: why your brain compares a tiny highlight of someone else's life to the whole story of your own, why social media makes the illusion stronger, and how borrowed goals quietly shape what you chase.

You'll learn:
- Why other people's lives look better from where you're standing
- How the highlight reel creates an unfair comparison
- Why the finish line keeps moving
- How to spot borrowed goals that were never yours
- The two questions that stop comparison from controlling you: "Behind according to who?" and "Am I doing better than I was?"

Think deeper. Live better. Become more.
